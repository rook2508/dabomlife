# Dabom

Dabom is a daily-life AI assistant being developed for older adults and their caregivers in South Korea.

The website includes an English-first, bilingual interactive demo, an accessible photo gallery, and a product and launch brief in PDF. Our development plan covers Claude conversations, speech-recognition integration, usability validation, and caregiver and service connections.

The interactive demo illustrates guidance and confirmation without making real payments, bookings, or caregiver messages. Its history is stored in the browser. Product images are AI-generated illustrations, rather than customer case studies.

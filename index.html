OUR STORY

Easier technology.
A more independent everyday.

Familiar tasks should not become
unfamiliar at the edge of a screen.

Ordering essentials, preparing for a hospital visit, finding out how to get a document. As digital services grow more capable, they can also bring more buttons and unfamiliar steps.

Dabom starts with a different question: what if the interface adapted to the person? We are building a way for older adults to express what they want, understand the next step, and make their own decisions.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you try it.

What can I try today?+

The English and Korean interactive demo includes voice/text input, rule-based daily-request routing, step-by-step guidance, simulated self/caregiver approval, local history, large text and mobile screens.

Does it place real orders or appointments?+

No. The current demo demonstrates preparation and confirmation. It does not search live services, place orders, take payments, book appointments, verify identity or submit public documents.

How will Dabom use Claude?+

We plan to use Claude to understand everyday requests, ask useful follow-up questions, and explain the next steps clearly. The interactive demo shows the guidance and review experience.

Are approval notifications sent to a caregiver?+

In this demo, request, approval and rejection roles are simulated within the same browser. Remote caregiver accounts and notifications are part of our development plan.

What happens to text and voice inputs?+

Demo history is stored locally in your browser and can be deleted there. Please use sample requests instead of sensitive information. Voice input uses supported browsers’ speech recognition and follows the browser provider’s processing policy.
